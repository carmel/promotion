# Privacy Policy - English Version

**Last Updated: October 4, 2025**

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how our Bible and Hymns application ("App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

By using this App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal identifying information such as:

- Names
- Email addresses
- Phone numbers
- Location data
- User accounts or profiles

### Non-Personal Information

The App may access the following device functions and data:

#### Storage Access

- **Purpose**: To store and retrieve hymn sheet music (PDF files) and music files (MP3 files) on your device
- **Data Type**: Hymn PDFs and MP3 audio files
- **Storage Location**: Local device storage only
- **Usage**: Files are stored locally on your device for offline access to hymns and music

#### Camera Access

- **Purpose**: To scan QR codes for quick access to hymns or content
- **Data Type**: QR code information only
- **Usage**: The camera is used solely for QR code scanning functionality. We do not capture, store, or transmit photos or videos

## How We Use Your Information

The information accessed by the App is used exclusively for:

1. **Storage Permission**:

   - Saving hymn sheet music (PDF format) to your device
   - Saving music files (MP3 format) to your device
   - Allowing you to access saved content offline
   - Managing your downloaded hymn library

2. **Camera Permission**:
   - Scanning QR codes to quickly access specific hymns or content
   - No images are stored or transmitted

## Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage Only**: All hymn PDFs and MP3 files are stored locally on your device
- **No Cloud Storage**: We do not upload your data to any cloud servers
- **No Data Transmission**: Downloaded content remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers or third parties
- **User Control**: You can delete downloaded files at any time through the App or your device's file manager

## Third-Party Services

This App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

This App does not knowingly collect any information from children. The App is designed to provide Bible reading and hymn access to users of all ages. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of the App.

## Permissions Required

### Android Permissions:

- **READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE**: To save and read hymn PDFs and MP3 files
- **CAMERA**: To scan QR codes for quick content access

### Why We Need These Permissions:

- **Storage**: Essential for downloading and storing hymn sheet music and audio files for offline use
- **Camera**: Optional feature for QR code scanning convenience

You can revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings, though this may limit App functionality.

## Data Retention

- All downloaded hymn PDFs and MP3 files remain on your device until you manually delete them
- Uninstalling the App will remove all associated data from your device
- We do not retain any of your data on our servers

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access all files downloaded through the App via your device's file manager
- Delete any or all downloaded content at any time
- Revoke storage and camera permissions in your device settings
- Uninstall the App and remove all associated data

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

- Posting the new Privacy Policy in the App
- Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy

Changes are effective immediately upon posting.

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not:

- Share your data with third parties
- Sell your information
- Use your data for advertising purposes
- Transmit your downloaded content to any servers

## International Users

This App can be used worldwide. All data remains stored locally on your device regardless of your location.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at:

**ArtisanHiram@outlook.com**  
**Hiram**

## Consent

By using this App, you consent to this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

# 隐私政策 - 中文版

**最后更新日期：2025 年 10 月 4 日**

## 引言

本隐私政策描述了我们的圣经赞美诗应用程序（"应用"、"我们"或"本应用"）如何收集、使用和保护您的信息。我们致力于保护您的隐私，并确保数据使用的透明度。

使用本应用即表示您同意按照本隐私政策收集和使用信息。

## 我们收集的信息

### 个人信息

我们不收集、存储或传输任何个人身份识别信息，包括：

- 姓名
- 电子邮件地址
- 电话号码
- 位置数据
- 用户账户或个人资料

### 非个人信息

本应用可能访问以下设备功能和数据：

#### 存储权限

- **用途**：在您的设备上存储和读取赞美诗歌谱（PDF 文件）和音乐文件（MP3 文件）
- **数据类型**：赞美诗 PDF 文件和 MP3 音频文件
- **存储位置**：仅限本地设备存储
- **使用方式**：文件仅存储在您的设备本地，以便离线访问赞美诗和音乐

#### 相机权限

- **用途**：扫描二维码以快速访问赞美诗或内容
- **数据类型**：仅二维码信息
- **使用方式**：相机仅用于二维码扫描功能。我们不会捕获、存储或传输照片或视频

## 我们如何使用您的信息

应用访问的信息仅用于：

1. **存储权限**：

   - 将赞美诗歌谱（PDF 格式）保存到您的设备
   - 将音乐文件（MP3 格式）保存到您的设备
   - 允许您离线访问已保存的内容
   - 管理您下载的赞美诗库

2. **相机权限**：
   - 扫描二维码以快速访问特定的赞美诗或内容
   - 不存储或传输任何图像

## 数据存储和安全

- **仅本地存储**：所有赞美诗 PDF 和 MP3 文件仅存储在您的设备本地
- **无云端存储**：我们不会将您的数据上传到任何云服务器
- **无数据传输**：下载的内容保留在您的设备上，不会传输到我们的服务器或第三方
- **用户控制**：您可以随时通过应用或设备的文件管理器删除已下载的文件

## 第三方服务

本应用不使用任何第三方分析、广告或跟踪服务。

## 儿童隐私

本应用不会故意收集儿童的任何信息。该应用旨在为所有年龄段的用户提供圣经阅读和赞美诗访问服务。建议家长和监护人监督儿童使用本应用。

- **读取/写入外部存储（READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE）**：用于保存和读取赞美诗 PDF 和 MP3 文件
